# Homa Bay Agronomy Bulletin 2026

Source IDs: `KMD-2026-10-14`, `KALRO-HB-2026-01`, `KALRO-HB-2026-02`, `KALRO-HB-2026-03`.

- **KALRO-HB-2026-01**: In germination maize during a dry spell, block nitrogen top-dressing and protect roots with hydrogel.
- **KALRO-HB-2026-02**: In flowering maize during a dry spell, use foliar potassium and targeted irrigation.
- **KALRO-HB-2026-03**: In waterlogged plots, open drainage and use a fungicide package.
